Our Story

Berua loves Italian food. Not in the abstract — in the specific. The weight of good dried pasta in your hand. The colour of properly ripe San Marzanos. The way a real Parmigiano smells when you crack it open. He's spent years finding producers he trusts and learning to make things by hand, properly.

Pasta 29 is his Italian delicatessen, coming to 29 Longbrooke Street, Exeter. Fresh pasta made daily. Antipasti prepared in-house. A carefully chosen pantry of ingredients worth caring about. Small, honest, and personal — the kind of place where the person behind the counter made everything on it.

Good food doesn't need to be complicated. It needs someone who cares enough to do it properly.
Berua, Founder
